6. Link Implementation Standards

Use normal crawlable anchor links.

Do not use JavaScript only navigation where a standard anchor is appropriate.

Use the canonical internal URL format already established by the website.

Do not introduce unnecessary trailing slash inconsistencies.

Do not open internal links in a new tab.

External map and reservation provider links may open in a new tab where appropriate.

Add accessible link labels where an icon or button does not clearly describe its destination.

Do not add links solely for SEO when they would feel unnatural to a visitor.

Do not link every occurrence of the same phrase. Use the most relevant contextual occurrence.
