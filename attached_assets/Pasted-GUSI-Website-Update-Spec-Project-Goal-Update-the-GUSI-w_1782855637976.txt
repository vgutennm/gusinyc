GUSI Website Update Spec

Project Goal

Update the GUSI website to clearly promote private events, celebrations, and group gatherings at the restaurant. The new content should help visitors immediately understand that GUSI can accommodate private events separately, including use of a private floor for up to 70 guests.

This update must support local SEO, AI SEO, and conversion by creating a dedicated Events page, adding homepage visibility, improving navigation, and adding internal links where relevant.

Important Context

Business Name: GUSI

Location: Greenwich Village, NYC

Audience: Russian speaking people from various cultures, as well as local NYC guests looking for private dining and event space

Primary Message: GUSI offers a private floor for events, celebrations, client meetings, personal gatherings, parties, corporate events, business functions, and special occasions for up to 70 guests.

Tone: Warm, elegant, inviting, hospitality focused, local, premium, and easy to understand.

Primary CTA: Reserve or Inquire About an Event

Important spelling correction: The customer wrote “privet events,” but the website should say “private events.”
